FAQ

What is a vocal remover, and how does it work?

A vocal remover is a tool that helps remove vocals from a song or separate vocals from instrumentals. People often use vocal removers to create karaoke tracks, extract acapellas, or prepare stems for remixing, editing, and content production.

To remove vocals, the tool analyzes the track and detects which parts of the audio belong to the human voice. It then separates the vocal layer from instruments such as drums, bass, guitar, and synths, as well as other elements in the mix.

LALAL.AI Vocal Remover is one example of an online service that can remove vocals, isolate vocals, extract various individual instruments, and split a track into vocal and instrumental stems.

How do I remove vocals from a song or video?

LALAL.AI Vocal Remover can be used to remove vocals from a song or video in just a few steps. You upload the file, the tool analyzes the audio, separates the vocal and instrumental parts, and then lets you download the versions you need.

  1. Open the LALAL.AI Vocal Remover and upload your audio or video file.
  2. Let the vocal remover analyze the track and detect the vocal and instrumental parts.
  3. Preview the separated result to check the vocal removal quality.
  4. Download the instrumental version if you want a track with vocals removed; download the vocal stem if you want to isolate the voice instead of removing it.
Can I remove lead vocals or backing vocals separately?

Yes, you can remove lead vocals or backing vocals separately with LALAL.AI Vocal Remover. When the Lead/back separation setting is enabled, the service separates the main vocal from the background vocal layers.

  1. Click the settings icon in the top-right corner of the upload widget.
  2. In the settings list, find Lead/back separation.
  3. Turn on the switch next to this setting.
  4. Upload your audio or video file.
  5. Wait for the track to be processed.
  6. Listen to the preview to evaluate the separation result.
  7. Download the tracks you need.

After processing, you can choose from four output tracks: Lead Vocal, Backing Vocal, Instrumental, and Instrumental + Backing.

What audio and video formats are supported?

LALAL.AI Vocal Remover supports multiple popular audio and video formats for online vocal removal and audio separation.

  • Audio formats: MP3, OGG, WAV, FLAC, AIFF, AAC, M4A.
  • Video formats: AVI, MP4, MKV, MOV, M4V.
How can I get better vocal removal results?

Getting better vocal removal results usually depends on the quality of the original file and the way the track was mixed. In general, a vocal remover works best when the vocals are clear, the instruments aren’t too heavily layered over the voice, and the source audio has minimal distortion or compression artifacts.

If you want to improve vocal removal results, it helps to:

  • Use a high-quality source file whenever possible.
  • Upload the full track instead of a heavily compressed excerpt.
  • Choose a version of the song with less background noise, clipping, or distortion.
  • Keep in mind that dense mixes with reverb, harmonies, and overlapping instruments may be harder to separate cleanly.
  • Preview the result before downloading to make sure the separation quality meets your needs.
  • Try a different neural network. Click the settings icon in the top-right corner of the upload widget, then select one of the available neural networks, regenerate the track snippets, and check the quality again.
How can I remove background noise and music?

Follow these steps to remove background noise and music with LALAL.AI Vocal Remover:

  1. Open the LALAL.AI Vocal Remover in your browser.
  2. In the upload widget, open the stem selection list and choose Voice and Noise.
  3. Click Select Files to upload your audio or video file.
  4. Preview the cleaned-up Voice track to check the noise removal result.
  5. If necessary, adjust the Noise Canceling Level in the settings.
  6. Click Split in Full to process the entire file.
  7. Download the Voice track with reduced background noise and music.
How many instruments can I remove or extract?

With LALAL.AI Vocal Remover, you can extract or remove multiple instruments and audio elements from a track by selecting the appropriate stem type. Available options include Vocal and Instrumental, Drums, Bass, Electric Guitar, Acoustic Guitar, Piano, Synthesizer, Voice and Noise, String Instruments, and Wind Instruments.

Can I change the output format of the track?

Yes, LALAL.AI Vocal Remover lets you change the output format of the processed track. By default, the output file keeps the same format as the uploaded file, but you can choose a different one before full processing begins.

How to change the output format in LALAL.AI:

  1. Open the LALAL.AI Vocal Remover in your browser.
  2. In the upload widget, select the stem you want to remove or extract.
  3. Click Select Files and upload your audio or video file.
  4. Wait for the separated track previews to appear.
  5. Under the previews, click the arrow next to the Split in Full button.
  6. Choose the output format you need from the drop-down list.
  7. Start full processing and download the result in the selected format.

For audio files, LALAL.AI Vocal Remover supports such output formats as MP3, OGG, AAC, AIFF, WAV, and FLAC. For video files, the result can stay in the original video format or be converted to one of the supported audio formats.

Can I reduce the echo in the voice or vocals?

Yes, LALAL.AI Vocal Remover can help reduce echo and reverb to improve speech clarity, clean up vocal tracks, and make spoken audio or singing easier to use for editing, publishing, or transcription.

How to reduce echo in vocals and voice recordings with LALAL.AI:

  1. Open the LALAL.AI Vocal Remover in your browser.
  2. In the upload widget, select either Vocal and Instrumental or Voice and Noise.
  3. Click the settings icon in the top-right corner of the upload widget.
  4. In the settings list, find De-echo.
  5. Turn on the switch next to this setting.
  6. Click Select Files and upload your audio or video file.
  7. Wait for the preview to appear.
  8. Listen to the result and check whether the voice sounds cleaner.
  9. Process the file in full and download the output with reduced echo and reverb.

How are the minutes deducted from my account?

There are several stem separation types you can choose from. One separation type is applied at a time, giving you two stems per file. Depending on the selected type, you receive a pair of vocal and instrumental stems, isolated drums + drumless stem, isolated bass + bassless stem, etc.

Minutes are deducted from the account by the following formula:

Total file length x stem separation type(s) number

Example:

File length: 5 minutes

Selected separation types: Drums, Piano and Vocal/Instrumental.

Total number of deducted minutes: 5 minutes x 3 stem separation types = 15 minutes

What are the Fast and Relaxed splitting modes?

Relaxed mode queues files for processing as server capacity allows (wait times vary by load);
Fast mode provides instant priority access for quicker results. Both deliver identical quality.

In Fast mode, you have a limited number of minutes per month. Unused Fast minutes reset at the start of each new month and do not roll over. Use them before renewal since the remaining time cannot be transferred or accumulated.

Note: You cannot manually switch modes. Instead, processing automatically starts in Fast mode at the beginning of each month until your minute limit is reached. Files then switch to Relaxed mode, where you can process unlimited minutes without restrictions.

Where can I see the remaining limit for splitting in the Fast mode?

All limits are displayed in your profile. On the site, hover the cursor over the minute balance at the top right corner of the page. It will expand into a window with information about your subscription and remaining minutes.

What if I run out of Fast minutes? Can I buy top-ups?

Yes, if your Fast minutes are depleted before the end of the month, you can purchase top-ups (additional minutes) for extra priority queue processing. Depending on your needs, you can get the Master top-up (750 minutes), Premium (3000 minutes), or Enterprise (5000 minutes) option.
